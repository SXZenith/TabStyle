# TabStyle

A clean, modern look for **TabInfo**'s Tab screen. Same stats, way easier to read, and you can always find yourself.

Press **Tab** like normal. TabStyle replaces TabInfo's window with its own.

## Features

- **6 layouts**, switch any time with the little buttons in the top right
  - **Split**: player list on the left, full stats for the selected player on the right
  - **Compare**: everyone side by side in columns
  - **Scoreboard**: one row per player with the key stats as columns; click a row for cards and every stat
  - **Dock**: a slim panel on the right edge so you can still see the game
  - **Classic**: the original TabInfo layout (team banners, every player's cards and stats in one list), rebuilt in the modern style
  - **Original TabInfo**: TabInfo's own window, untouched, with small buttons in its title bar to switch back
- **6 color themes**: Midnight, Ember, Neon, Crimson, Mono, Arctic (light)
- **You're always easy to find**: highlighted and tagged **YOU**, and with the **YOU FIRST** toggle (on by default) you're pinned to the top / left of every list
- **Cards toggle** (top right, off by default): shows each player's cards as a scrollable strip. Hover a card to see the full card, just like TabInfo. Scroll the wheel or drag to move through any number of cards
- **Collapsible stat sections**, remembered between games
- Remembers your layout, colors, and toggles
- Comes with **CatInfo** for the full set of stats (bullets, bounces, life steal, gun and character stats and more), and works with **every other mod that adds stats to TabInfo**
- Esc closes the Tab screen
- Lighter than the original: stats refresh a few times a second instead of every frame

## Notes

- Which stats show is still controlled by TabInfo's (and CatInfo's) own settings (main menu > **Mod Options**).
- Client-side only. Other players don't need it.
